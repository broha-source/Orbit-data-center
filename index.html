QUALITY PRINCIPLE

Failure flags may overlap: a tile can carry more than one flag. Automatic gates only judge the generated results and do not influence generation. Geometry gates are a pre-screening step; CFD/CHT remains a separate validation step.

Conditional diffusion pipeline

Diffusion workflow,
step by step.

01PREPARE
5 × 2,000

Build the SDF dataset

2,000 SDF tiles per family × 5 families = 10,000 training geometries. Each tile is 256 × 256. SDF is the signed distance to the channel wall: positive inside the channel, negative outside.

02TRAIN

One conditional model

One conditional diffusion U-Net learns all five families for 50 epochs. Family + channel width conditions are fed to every layer. Boundary Weighting counts pixels near channel walls up to 5× more. Corner·Thickness Weighting counts sharp corners and thin walls up to 13× more for Serpentine and Spiral.

03ADAPT
5 · 7 · 9 · 11 · 13 passes

Pass-Count Conditioning

For Serpentine only, the trained model is frozen and a small add-on condition specifies 5, 7, 9, 11 or 13 passes. It is trained on 2,000 Serpentine tiles. It is switched off for the other four families, whose outputs remain identical to the frozen model.

04GENERATE

Turn noise into an SDF

DDIM uses 100 denoising steps instead of 1,000, with no retraining needed. Generating 1,000 tiles per family — 5,000 in total — takes about 80 minutes.

05VERIFY
SDF > 0→PASS / FAIL

Threshold, then gate

SDF > 0 becomes the fluid mask. Automatic gates check inlet-to-outlet connectivity, minimum wall thickness and channel structure, then report PASS or FAIL. Gates only judge results; CFD/CHT remains a separate validation step.

Why Serpentine dropped

Training tiles have only odd pass counts, but the model was never told the count. It sometimes drew even counts or misplaced turns. Of 192 failures, 163 had a wrong pass structure. Even-count tiles failed 64.5% of the time, versus 13.6% for odd-count tiles.

Dark red in the Corner·Thickness Weighting figures marks extra weight. Red in the failed generated tile highlights where the pass structure goes wrong.
